A history nobody
kept under control.

The (attempt at a) full chronology is here, if something is missing, please alert an official player in the Discord server. Summary: IsraelCraftMPG came first, the IsraelCraft identity grew from it, and the world accumulated wars, crime, politics, builds, etc...

FOUNDING ERA

IsraelCraftMPG is created

Big P created the server. Glenn and Max are also recognized as founders. The exact date is unknown, however it occured in mid to late April, 2026. This began the original world and a long, in-depth history.

THE NEW ERA

The IsraelCraft identity takes over

Across some platforms, IsraelCraftMPG was rebranded more simply as IsraelCraft. A knew server was created, and the hosting platform moved from Aternos to Exaroton. The civilization kept the same deeply normal tradition of not being normal. Leif also joined the server at this time as Bedrock compatibility was introduced.

7/19/2026

First Larpville Games

The First Larpville Games, only containing "The OG 5", its only participants were Glenn and Leif, there were no prizes, however it was an integral part of the server's history.

8/16/2026

Ratification of Constitution

The consitution becomes official. The 4 founders of the new era sign it off.

8/18/2026

First Official Larpville Games

The recurring player-created sporting institution becomes official. Max places first, Glenn second, Pierson third, and Leif fourth.

Law of the land

Don't ruin the server.

IsraelCraft is vanilla survival with room for quality-of-life improvements, not a race to see who can install the most unfair client.

No griefing.
Whitelist exists specifically to keep random griefers out.
No X-ray.
Resource-finding cheats and similarly extreme advantages are not allowed.
QoL mods are okay.
Convenience is fine so long as it does not become an extreme gameplay advantage.
Vanilla survival spirit.
The point is to play the world, build things, make history and occasionally accuse Max of crimes.
